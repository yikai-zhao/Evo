Evo Privacy Policy

Evo does not require an account or collect your real name, email address, or payment details. Local progress is saved in your browser via localStorage.

In multiplayer mode, your chosen nickname and live gameplay state (position, HP, rank, and species) are temporarily relayed so other players can see you in match sessions. The relay necessarily receives connection metadata such as an IP address but does not write it to gameplay records.

Anonymous aggregate events such as run completion, ad offer display, ad completion, and revive usage are counted to measure reliability and game balance. These aggregates do not contain nickname, chat text, or account identifiers.

When embedded on platforms (such as Poki/CrazyGames), ad services may apply their own privacy policies.
